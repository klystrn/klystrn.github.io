# Watches (easter egg)

> I wear a watch in every professional setting from NP onwards (classroom, office, HQ Guards) —
> never in the training and combatant montage. The **first and last** time each watch appears in
> the comic, hovering (or tapping) it highlights the watch with a shadowy shimmer and shows the
> comment below. Where a watch appears is set in the storyboards with `watch X Y <id>`; the build
> works out first and last sightings. Edit the comments here. Arcs can add their own watches later.

## Watch tissot-prx · Tissot PRX Powermatic 80
- era: J.P. Morgan internship (2024–2025)
- look: steel case, rose-gold bezel, white waffle ("tapisserie") dial, integrated bracelet [EDIT: confirm size (35 or 40 mm) and reference]
- comment: The first watch I ever bought with my own money, and it became my daily beater. I fell in love with the waffle dial, and with how the Powermatic 80 movement runs for 80 hours — about double what a typical automatic gives you.

## Watch hamilton-murph · Hamilton Khaki Field Murph
- era: National Service, HQ Guards (2025–2027)
- look: black dial, steel case, field-watch numerals, needle seconds hand [EDIT: confirm 38 or 42 mm — the Morse-code seconds hand is on the 42 mm]
- comment: The perfect blend of elegance, cultural relevance and utilitarian design. Fun fact: "Eureka" is printed in Morse code along the seconds hand — what Murph shouts in Interstellar when she solves the gravity equation with the data sent through her watch's second hand.

## Watch vc-fiftysix · Vacheron Constantin FiftySix
- era: RegiStudio (2026–present)
- look: steel FiftySix self-winding, 40 mm, open caseback showing the Maltese cross rotor (ref. 4600E/000A-B487)
- comment: The first watch my father gave me, for my 19th birthday. An elegant, subtle dress watch for a collector's eye: it draws on the 6073 from 1956, and the open caseback shows the signature Maltese cross. It has come to mean a lot to me — a family legacy I'll one day pass down to my own children.
