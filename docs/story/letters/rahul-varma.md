LETTER OF RECOMMENDATION

It is a pleasure to provide this testimonial for Reginald, who served as a software engineering intern at J.P. Morgan from April 2024 to March 2025. He quickly established himself as a Subject Matter Expert on workload testing and data visualization. His technical proficiency in Python and SQL was evident in the tools he built, specifically an automated server workload report that identified misallocated resources and visualized optimization opportunities. His work contributed significantly to improving our operational efficiency and accelerated our multimillion USD project.

Beyond his technical contributions, Reginald demonstrated maturity, leadership, and teamwork far beyond the typical intern level. He effectively spearheaded our team’s Jira sprint board, creating and tracking thousands of issues. He also assisted in building a CEM Cloud 101 guide which was presented to global participants. His ability to collaborate across our offices in Singapore, Mumbai, and the US ensured that cross-functional tasks were executed seamlessly.

Reginald possesses a rare blend of technical rigor, proactive problem solving, and professional communication. He is a disciplined and high-performing individual who would be a tremendous addition to any world-class engineering team. I strongly recommend him without reservation.

Sincerely,

Rahul Varma
